Privacy notice.

This notice describes the current website and the business inquiries you choose to send to Capraz LLC.

Visiting this website

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses and request details, to operate and secure its hosting service. More information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We have not added advertising trackers, analytics scripts, embedded social widgets or tracking cookies to this website. The website does not save preferences or inquiry content in your browser. Fonts and images are served with the website.

Contacting us

You may contact us by email or through the wholesale inquiry form. The form collects your name, company or brand, business email, optional website and message. Email links open your email application.

We use business correspondence to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential partnerships and manage ongoing business relationships. Email is handled through our existing business email service. Relevant records may be kept while needed for the relationship and applicable record-keeping requirements. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Inquiry form providers

The form uses Cloudflare Pages Functions to validate and process submissions, and Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated abuse, alongside request limits and a hidden spam-prevention field. Inquiry notifications are sent through Resend to our business email address. We use Cloudflare D1 to keep pseudonymous security counters containing a keyed network identifier, time window and attempt count, without storing your message in that database. Older counters are removed during subsequent requests; Cloudflare may retain service records and backups under its policies. Cloudflare and Resend may process inquiry content and connection or delivery information to provide and secure these services. Turnstile may process browser and device signals for security verification; more information is available in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Resend processes and retains delivery records under its service policies. We do not use the form to subscribe you to marketing emails. Form content is not intentionally saved in your browser or written to application logs.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent us, contact support@caprazllc.com. Requests are considered subject to applicable obligations and the information needed to verify and handle the request.
