05A little clarity

Before we
say hello.

What kind of partnerships do you look for?+

We welcome conversations with brands and their designated distributors about authorized retail and long-term wholesale relationships. Product fit, market scope and commercial terms are discussed together.

Does my brand stay in control?+

Authorization, applicable brand policies and marketplace permissions define our work. We agree the scope before moving forward and discuss additional services with you first.

Can you help with brand protection?+

With your authorization and appropriate platform access, we can help document suspected misuse and submit reports. Marketplaces review reports and determine outcomes; seller removal is not guaranteed.

How do we get started?+

Email support@caprazllc.com with a short introduction to your brand, your website or catalog and the markets you are interested in. We can then arrange a conversation about the next steps.
